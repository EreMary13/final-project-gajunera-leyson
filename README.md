# Student Payment Organizations and Tracking System (SPOTS)
Centralized student fee tracking and automated clearance verification, without manual spreadsheets.

## Problem
- School admins and student organization treasurers struggle to track student fee payments manually, leading to lost records, errors, and delays during school clearance.
- College students lack a centralized, real-time way to verify which school-wide or department fees they have paid or still owe.

## Target Users
College Students; Institute/Department Treasurers; School Admins

## Solution
A centralized web platform where School Admins post campus-wide fees, Institute Treasurers manage department-specific obligations via a one-click payment checklist, and students track their real-time payment and clearance standing.

## Main Feature
Students can view and track their required fees and real-time clearance status, while officers verify payments with a one-click checklist that automatically updates clearance eligibility.

## Pages
- Homepage / Login: Role-based login and registration portal for Students, Institute Treasurers, and School Admins.
- App Page (Student Dashboard): Displays categorized school-wide and institutional fees, payment statuses, digital receipts, and an overall clearance status banner.
- Officer Dashboard: Displays collection statistics, student counts, and institute filters for treasurers and admins to monitor financial progress.
- Fee Posting & Verification Page: Forms for posting new school-wide or departmental fees alongside a searchable student masterlist with one-click payment toggles and automatic clearance calculation.
